# 🚀 Hackathon Demo Survival Checklist

Keep this checklist handy 1 hour before pitching to judges.

---

### ⏱️ T-minus 60 Minutes (Technical Polish)
- [ ] **Feature Freeze**: Stop writing new features. Polish what you have.
- [ ] **Test Demo Flow 3 Times**: Run through the exact clicks you will make on stage without changing tabs.
- [ ] **Enable Fallback Mock**: Ensure `ENABLE_MOCK_FALLBACK="true"` in `.env.local` so if Wi-Fi degrades, your demo still responds instantly.
- [ ] **Record 90-Second Backup Video**: Record screen (OBS / Loom) showing the full working flow. If laptop/projector fails, play the video immediately.

---

### 📶 T-minus 30 Minutes (Environment & Hardware)
- [ ] **Mobile Hotspot On Standby**: Conference venue Wi-Fi almost always slows down during judging. Have your phone hotspot paired.
- [ ] **Close Background Apps**: Close Discord, heavy tabs, Slack notifications, and IDE watchers to avoid embarrassing pop-ups.
- [ ] **Zoom In Browser to 110%-125%**: Projectors and judges' screens are often low-res. Zooming in makes typography and metrics crisp and legible from 5 feet away.
- [ ] **Full Screen Mode (F11)**: Pitching in full screen looks like a finished product rather than localhost in Chrome.

---

### 🎤 T-minus 10 Minutes (Pitch Alignment)
- [ ] **First Speaker Identified**: Decide who speaks the first sentence. Never start with awkward *"Who goes first?"*.
- [ ] **Timekeeper Set**: One teammate quietly tracks the 3-minute stopwatch.
- [ ] **Smile & High Energy**: Confidence and enthusiasm are contagious!
